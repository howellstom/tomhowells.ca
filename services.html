Services

Cohabitation Agreements

Navigating the complexities of cohabitation requires careful consideration and planning. I offer comprehensive legal guidance on cohabitation agreements to unmarried couples who are living together or planning to do so. Whether you're looking to establish financial boundaries, clarify property rights, or protect assets, I provide tailored agreements that reflect your unique needs and circumstances.

Separation Agreements

When a relationship comes to an end, it's crucial to have a clear and legally binding separation agreement in place. I assist couples in crafting fair and equitable separation agreements that address key issues such as division of assets, spousal support, and parenting arrangements. With sensitivity and expertise, I strive to facilitate amicable resolutions while safeguarding my clients' rights and interests.

Parenting Plan Agreements

Putting the well-being of your children first is paramount during and after a separation or divorce. My parenting plan agreements are designed to establish effective co-parenting arrangements that prioritize the needs of your children. From defining decision-making responsibilities to outlining parenting schedules, I work closely with my clients to develop customized plans that promote stability and harmony for their families.

Litigation

In situations where negotiation and mediation are not viable options, I provide robust representation in family law court proceedings. With experience advocating for clients in litigation matters, I offer strategic counsel and assertive advocacy to protect your rights and pursue favourable outcomes. Whether your case involves complex asset division, contested parenting disputes, or other contentious issues, I am committed to providing diligent and effective representation every step of the way.

Areas of Focus

Decision-Making Responsibility and Parenting Time

Determining decision-making responsibility and parenting time arrangements requires careful consideration of various factors, including the best interests of the child. I help parents navigate these issues with compassion and expertise, and work tirelessly to develop parenting plans that promote stability, consistency, and positive relationships between children and both parents.

Child Support

Ensuring that children receive the financial support they need is essential for their well-being. I offer comprehensive guidance on child support matters, including calculating support payments, modifying existing orders, and enforcing support obligations. I advocate vigorously to secure fair and equitable support arrangements that prioritize the welfare of the children involved.

Spousal Support

Spousal support plays a crucial role in providing financial security for individuals following a separation or divorce. I assist clients in navigating spousal support issues, including determining entitlement, calculating support amounts, and negotiating or litigating support agreements. Whether you're seeking support or defending against a spousal support claim, I provide strategic advice and representation tailored to your specific circumstances.
